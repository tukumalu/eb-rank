## What the sources agree on (5 bullets)

- Under 100 USD now buys real ANC plus LDAC plus app EQ; reviewers no longer treat these as flagship-only features (RTINGS Sep 2026, SoundGuys Oct 2026).
- Anker Soundcore owns the value-ANC center: Space A40 as RTINGS overall top pick and Liberty 4 NC as SoundGuys best-ANC pick is a cross-source consensus, not one outlet.
- EarFun Air Pro 4 Plus is the feature-density leader (dual driver, BT 6.0, LDAC plus aptX Lossless, 10-band EQ) with only-average ANC (SoundGuys lab 80pct and owner-note alignment).
- No-ANC buds (Sony WF-C510, Pixel A-Series) still win on simplicity, fit, and battery-per-dollar but cannot rank top on a 20pct ANC weight (What Hi-Fi, TechRadar, SoundGuys, Google specs alignment).
- iPhone users get AAC-only on every top-5 Android-leaning bud; no H1/H2, no Apple Find My, no head-tracked Apple Spatial in this price class (maker spec pages plus RTINGS iPhone pick being the W1 neckband Beats Flex, not a true-wireless ANC bud).

## Where sources conflict or are thin

- Overall winner differs by outlet weighting: RTINGS weights all-round ANC plus comfort toward Space A40; SoundGuys weights feature newness toward EarFun Air Pro 4 Plus. This report weights battery plus ANC heavily, so Anker tops; a sound-tech weight would put EarFun first. Method, not fact, drives the split.
- Battery numbers conflict by condition: maker 10/50h CLAIMED vs LDAC-plus-ANC 5/25h tested PARTIAL for Liberty; maker up-to-10h CLAIMED vs 6h-plus continuous PARTIAL for Nothing 3a. Always compare ANC-on tested to ANC-on tested, not maker max to lab ANC-on.
- Thin: exact ANC topology (hybrid vs feedforward vs feedback) is rarely disclosed; most maker pages say Adaptive or Smart without topology, so subtypes above are PARTIAL except SoundPEATS hybrid and JLab feed-forward which are VERIFIED. Exact driver sizes for Space A40, CMF 2 Plus, JBL, and Sony were NOT FOUND in allowed snippets opened and are left blank rather than guessed.
- Thin: long-term degradation, connection-drop rates, and warranty-claim rates have no allowed-source datasets in this pass; all durability scores use build, IP, mic/call stability, and battery-test depth as proxies, plus RTINGS comfort/build notes. Treat durability 7-8 as lab-plus-design proxy, not failure-rate data.
- Thin: MSRP vs street: Anker, Sony, and CMF street often under MSRP; 2.5M VND equivalence moves with FX and is explicitly left to the shopee lane per brief. No VND conversion is given here.
