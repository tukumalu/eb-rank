# Shopee Vietnam availability and pricing for top budget earbuds (15 Nov 2026)

## Method and evidence limits

- Searched in Vietnamese first on shopee.vn via web search + cached Shopee listing renders, viewed 15 Nov 2026 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh). Wrote short rows only; no raw dumps kept.
- The earbuds lane file `eb-earbuds.md` landed mid-pass (12:04 PM). Started from a 7-model proxy shortlist, then extended coverage to all 5 of its ranked top models: Liberty 4 NC (already covered), Space A40, CMF Buds 2 Plus, EarFun Air Pro 4 Plus, Nothing Ear (3a). 11 rows total.
- Firecrawl credits were exhausted (0 left, reset Dec 2026), so no Firecrawl scrapes; evidence comes from search-service Shopee page renders plus Vietnamese retailer/aggregator anchors. Direct HTTP fetch of Shopee returns a bot-wall page (identical 161625-byte shell, no price fields), so figures below are from rendered caches, not live checkout.
- Labels: VERIFIED = seen on the listing render with URL + date; PARTIAL = seen but stale/ambiguous/proxy; CLAIMED = seller's own title text only; NOT FOUND = not visible in captured evidence.

## Red flags

- JBL Tune Beam at 2,930,000 on Shopee vs 1,550,000 retail (CellphoneS via aggregators): stale or overpriced listing, and it is sold out. Do not buy. (VERIFIED Shopee side, PARTIAL retail side.)
- Buds FE: both Shopee listings found carry sold-out flags; availability is the risk, not price. Prefer offline/retail stock. (VERIFIED flags.)
- Space A40 search results mix new, likenew, and spare-part listings (single earbud, used charging case); buy new-only and confirm the seal. (VERIFIED search-result mix.)
- CMF Buds 2 Plus and Space A40 have no official-store Shopee listing in captured evidence; counterfeit/grey risk is highest on exactly these two. Stick to Mall sellers and verify ratings live. (Assessment; no fake unit verified.)
- Sold counts and ship-from cities are invisible in captured renders, so do not trust popularity or location claims in titles without a live check. (NOT FOUND.)
- Voucher/after-voucher prices (R50i 385,560; Beam 2 2,224,000; Buds FE 1,631,800; Nothing 3a 2,312,000) are campaign prices and may not reproduce at checkout. (VERIFIED as displayed.)

## What the sources agree on (5 bullets)

- 10 of 11 rows have at least one Shopee Vietnam listing with a stated price; the CMF row has a price but with a sold-out flag. (VERIFIED.)
- Brand-official Shopee Mall stores exist for Anker, Xiaomi, EarFun, and Nothing; Sony/JBL/Samsung coverage runs through big-retailer Mall stores (Hoang Ha, Long Hung, SES). (VERIFIED.)
- Official-store prices sit under 2.5M VND except the stale Beam 1 listing. (VERIFIED.)
- Every captured official listing shows 100pct-genuine + free-shipping badges. (VERIFIED.)
- Warranty text is mostly absent from official listing renders; the only on-page warranties seen are JBL Beam 2 BH 12 thang at Hoang Ha and Nothing/EarFun reseller title claims. (VERIFIED + CLAIMED as labeled.)

## Where sources conflict or are thin

- WF-C510: aggregator shows a 790,000 TGDD imei price vs 1,100,000 Shopee Hoang Ha vs 1,365,000 TGDD list. The 790k line is PARTIAL/unreliable; use 1.1M as the working price.
- Beam 1 vs Beam 2 pricing is scattered (1.55M retail Beam 1, 2.05M+ retail Beam 2, 2.224M Shopee Beam 2, 2.93M stale Beam 1). Normalise on Beam 2 at 2.1-2.3M.
- Sold units, ship-from location (except Nothing HCMC), and delivery time are NOT FOUND for almost every model in captured evidence; popularity ranking cannot be verified from this pass.
- Space A40 and CMF Buds 2 Plus evidence is the thinnest: reseller-only, sold-out flags, no Mall badge, no captured shop rating for CMF.
- EarFun naming (Air Pro 4 vs 4+ vs 4 Plus) is inconsistent across official-store renders and reseller titles; confirm the exact variant (Plus, Bluetooth 6.0 class per earbuds lane) at checkout.
